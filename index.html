serum2vital: hear the conversions

serum2vital converts Xfer Serum presets into Vital presets. These pages play the Serum original next to the converted preset in Vital, rendered headlessly from the same MIDI phrase, so you can judge how close a conversion gets before installing anything.

  • Reese 94 presets, 81 with a Serum original
  • Reese Lead 1 presets, 1 with a Serum original

Renders are demos of the conversion only. The presets belong to their respective authors.
