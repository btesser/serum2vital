serum2vital: hear the conversions

serum2vital converts Xfer Serum presets into Vital presets. These pages play the Serum original next to the converted preset in Vital, rendered headlessly from the same MIDI phrase, with the converter's notes and closeness metrics for every preset, so you can judge how close a conversion gets and where it falls short.
