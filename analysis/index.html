How close are the conversions?

645 presets scored against their Serum render (0 had no Serum clip). Distance = mean |dB| difference over 1/6-octave bands (whole clip) + |level offset in dB|; lower is closer. env corr is the correlation of the two loudness envelopes (20 ms windows), 1 = same shape in time.

Overall median distance 13.53 (spectral 8.84 dB, level 4.25 dB), median envelope correlation 0.706; 10 presets within 3. Level offset (Vital minus Serum): median -0.65 dB; 260 presets Vital louder by more than 1 dB, 304 quieter, 81 within 1 dB.

By tier

clean = no notes other than two constant ones (old-build switch block defaults, Plate reverb); common fx = additionally only the reverb-by-ear and compressor notes; approximated = anything else. If the clean tier is not near exact, the gap is in the core mapping (oscillators, filters, envelopes, levels), not in any listed approximation.
