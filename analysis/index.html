669 presets scored against their Serum render (0 had no Serum clip). Distance = mean |dB| difference over 1/6-octave bands (whole clip) + |level offset in dB|; lower is closer. env corr is the correlation of the two loudness envelopes (20 ms windows), 1 = same shape in time.
